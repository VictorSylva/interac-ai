## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### How do content updates work?
Once your website is deployed, you can request text changes, service updates, and image swaps directly from your InteracAI dashboard. Future content updates are handled through the InteracAI platform without extra development fees.

#### Can I use my custom domain?
Yes! Both Growth and Pro plans support custom domain configurations. If you own a domain (e.g. `mybusiness.com`), our team will help point DNS records to point it to your website. Note that managed hosting and SSL are only provided on the Pro plan; Growth plan users host their site externally.

#### Is hosting paid separately?
No. Standard cloud hosting and security certificates (SSL) are completely included in your monthly Pro subscription. For the Growth plan, users host their website externally, or can upgrade to Pro to include managed hosting. If you cancel your Pro subscription, managed hosting will be deactivated.

#### What happens if I exceed my AI conversation limits?
If you exceed your plan's monthly conversation limits, your AI receptionists will stop responding. You can purchase Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) token refills at any time. These tokens never expire and are only consumed after your monthly subscription quota is exhausted.
