# INTERACAI — INTEGRATION ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE
**Jargon-Free Customer Onboarding, Software Discovery & Connection Journey**

---

## 1. The Core Onboarding Philosophy

When a business owner, hotel manager, school principal, or doctor signs up for InteracAI, they should **never** be confronted with technical jargon such as "REST Webhooks", "JSON Schemas", or "API Endpoints".

The onboarding journey must communicate one crystal-clear promise:
> **"InteracAI does not replace the software you already trust. We connect your existing systems and turn their daily events into real-time business intelligence and automated team coordination."**

### Step 3: Fast & Secure Authorization
For each selected software, InteracAI presents a clean, non-technical setup card:

#### Scenario A: Direct Cloud Integration (e.g. WhatsApp, Paystack)
> **Connect Paystack**:
> *"Enter your Paystack Secret Key (starts with `sk_live_...`). InteracAI encrypts this key with bank-grade AES encryption to verify payments and automate customer receipts."*
> `[ Input Secret Key: ******************************** ] [ Save & Connect ]`

#### Scenario B: Inbound Webhook Connection (e.g. Mews, Shopify, Cloudbeds)
> **Connect Mews / Cloudbeds**:
> *"Copy this unique Webhook URL and paste it into your Mews Settings -> Integrations page:"*
> `[ https://api.interacai.com/api/v1/hotel/integrations/webhook?org_id=org_9941a ] [ Copy Link ]`
> *"InteracAI will automatically receive your bookings, check-ins, and room changes."*

#### Scenario C: Spreadsheets & Legacy Files (e.g. Excel / CSV)
> **Upload Your Existing Data**:
> *"Drag and drop your exported guest list, student roster, or product price list. InteracAI will import your existing records in 5 seconds."*
> `[ Drag & Drop .CSV or .XLSX file here ]`
