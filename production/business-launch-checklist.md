# Business Launch Checklist & Go-to-Market Strategy

This document outlines the business onboarding flow and the strategy for acquiring the first paying customers.

## 1. Optimized Onboarding Flow
To maximize conversion, the onboarding experience must be frictionless:
1. **Sign Up**: Email/Password via Firebase.
2. **Organization Creation**: Define the Business Name and Industry.
3. **Plan Selection**: Present 3 clear tiers (e.g., Starter, Pro, Enterprise).
4. **Payment Gateway**: Seamless redirect to Paystack checkout or inline payment.
5. **WhatsApp Integration Setup**: A simple wizard to connect the business's WhatsApp number.
6. **First Workflow Template**: Instead of a blank slate, pre-load an industry-specific automation template (e.g., "Customer FAQ Auto-Reply").

## 2. First Paying Customer Strategy

### Priority Verticals
Based on current capabilities (Automations, WhatsApp, Cameras), target these specific industries first:
1. **Retail Stores / Supermarkets**: High volume of WhatsApp inquiries about stock and hours. Argus OS integration for foot traffic / security.
2. **Hospitals / Clinics**: Appointment booking automations via WhatsApp.
3. **Real Estate / Residential Estates**: Access control, visitor logging via WhatsApp bots and camera verification.

### Go-to-Market Actions
- **Offer White-Glove Onboarding**: For the first 10 customers, manually set up their workflows to guarantee success and gather direct feedback.
- **Start with WhatsApp Modules**: Enable the WhatsApp automation modules first. This provides the most immediate, tangible value to small-medium businesses over complex analytics dashboards.
- **Case Studies**: Turn the first 3 successful deployments into video case studies to drive future sales.
